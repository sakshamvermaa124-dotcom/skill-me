How to Complete Your Tasks & Submit for Review

Follow these simple steps every week to complete your assigned task, share your work on LinkedIn, and get it approved by an admin.

1
Open Your Assigned Task
Go to the Submissions tab and open the task assigned for the current week. Read the description and deliverables carefully before you begin.
2
Complete the Task
Build/solve the task as described. Keep a record of what you built so you can talk about it in your post.
3
Post About It on LinkedIn
Use the suggested caption on each task card (copy it with one click), publish a LinkedIn post about your progress, and tag @SkillMe.
4
Submit Your Post URL for Review
Paste the link to your published LinkedIn post into the task card and click "Submit for Review". An admin will review it and approve it — your progress updates automatically once approved.

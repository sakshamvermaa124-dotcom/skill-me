Help

How weekly tasks work, tips for getting approved, and useful links.

How to complete a task

1
Open this week's task
Go to Tasks and pick the current week. Read the brief and deliverables before you start.
2
Do the work
Code, notebook, Figma design or cloud setup — whatever the task asks for. Grab screenshots or a short screen recording as you go.
3
Post it on LinkedIn
Copy the suggested caption from the task, add your screenshots or demo, and tag @SkillMe. Put your GitHub, Figma, notebook or live-site link in the post or its first comment.
4
Submit the post link
On your post, click ⋯ → Copy link to post, paste it into the task and press Submit for Review. Your progress updates automatically once an admin approves it.

Tips for getting approved

Paste the post link, not your profile

Links ending in /in/your-name are profile links. Use ⋯ → Copy link to post on the post itself.

Rejected? Just resubmit

Read the admin note on the task, fix your post and paste the new link. Only rejected tasks accept a new link.

3 approvals unlock credentials

Once 3 of your 4 tasks are approved, your Certificate, LOR and Portfolio unlock under Credentials.

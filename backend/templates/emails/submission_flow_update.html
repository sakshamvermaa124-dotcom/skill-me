✅ What You Need to Do Now

1
Open your Student Dashboard

2
Build it locally
Complete the week's project on your machine (VS Code / localhost) — no repo or PR needed.
3
Post about it on LinkedIn
Use the ready-made caption on your dashboard (or write your own), share a screenshot or short demo, and make sure to tag @SkillMe.
4
Submit your post URL for review
Paste your LinkedIn post link into the submission box for that week on your dashboard. Our team reviews it and marks it Approved or Rejected (with a note if changes are needed — you can resubmit).

    Your privacy is critically important to us. SkillMe is an enterprise registered under the Ministry of MSME, Govt. of India (Udyam Reg. No. UDYAM-UP-50-0294192). This Privacy Policy outlines how SkillMe ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information.

    1. Information We Collect

    When you apply for our internship or use our platform, we may collect the following information:

    • Personal identification information (Name, Email address, Phone number)
    • Professional information (LinkedIn profile, Resume)
    • Payment information (Processed securely via Razorpay; we do not store your full card details)

    2. How We Use Your Information

    We use the collected information for various purposes:

    • To process your internship application and create your dashboard
    • To track your progress via your verified LinkedIn submissions
    • To issue your Certificate of Completion and Letter of Recommendation
    • To process payments via our payment gateway (Razorpay)
    • To send you important notifications regarding your internship

    3. Data Protection and Sharing

    We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers (such as Razorpay for payments and email providers for notifications) strictly for the purpose of operating our service.

    4. Cookies

    We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our platform and hold certain information to improve your user experience.

    5. Your Rights

    You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you and to ask that your personal data be corrected, updated, or deleted. Please contact our support team to exercise these rights.

01What are the requirements for this internship?

Basic knowledge of your chosen track and a short screening quiz — that's it. Once you're in, you work remotely at your own pace, build each week's milestone and submit it as a LinkedIn post. Your verified certificate + LOR unlock at the end, once 3 of your 4 weekly milestones are approved.

Screening quiz100% remote
02Does everyone who applies get in?

No. Anyone can apply and we encourage every student to, but selection is competitive. Every internship request is read by our team and we shortlist the students we think will complete all four weeks. The quiz checks your basics, but your answer to “Why do you want to join?” matters just as much, so take it seriously. If you're shortlisted, you'll hear from us within 48 hours.

Hand-reviewedShortlist
03How is my performance evaluated?

Every weekly submission is reviewed against that week's acceptance criteria by the SkillMe team, which includes engineers who work at Walmart, Google and LinkedIn. Submit mindfully. Your progress is the number of approved milestones out of 4. Top performers are highlighted to our hiring partners for direct referrals.

Weekly review4 milestones
04How much time does it take?

The internship is 4 weeks long, with one project milestone per week. You work at your own pace within your 4-week window. Most students spend 2–3 hours a week on tasks — it's fully remote and flexible.

4 weeksFlexible hours
05Do I need prior experience to apply?

Basic knowledge of your chosen domain is recommended (fundamentals, not expert level). The screening quiz covers basics to make sure you're ready. You don't need prior internship experience — that's exactly what SkillMe provides.

Beginner friendlyDomain basics
06How do the weekly tasks work?

Each week has one milestone on your project, with clear requirements and acceptance criteria in your dashboard. Build it, share it as a LinkedIn post using the caption we provide, and paste the post link into your dashboard. The SkillMe team reviews it and approves the milestone or tells you what to fix.

Verified tasksReal projects
07How is the certificate verified?

Every certificate has a unique ID and QR code. Anyone — a recruiter, college, or employer — can visit our verification page, enter the ID, and see your certificate details including name, domain, issue date, and duration. It's fully tamper-proof and publicly verifiable.

QR verifiableTamper-proof
08What does the placement assistance include?

Top interns get access to our hiring partner network — startups and product companies that actively recruit from SkillMe. Top performers also get direct referrals from our team to relevant openings and a Letter of Recommendation (LOR).

Hiring partnersDirect referrals
09Is this a paid internship? Is there a stipend?

Top performers earn a stipend of up to $100 a month. It's performance-based and isn't paid to every intern: it goes to the interns whose reviewed work stands out in their cohort. Every intern who is selected still gets an offer letter, reviewed milestones and a public portfolio.

Up to $100/monthTop performers
10Can I do internships in multiple tracks?

Absolutely. Each domain is an independent internship with its own timeline, projects, and certificate. Many students do 2–3 domains to build a diverse project portfolio. Each one adds a new set of approved milestones and a new certificate to your profile.

Multi-trackDiverse portfolio
11Is SkillMe a real company? Is this internship legit?

Yes. SkillMe is registered with the Ministry of MSME, Government of India (Udyam registration UDYAM-UP-50-0294192) and is based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. You can check us before you apply: verify any SkillMe certificate on our verification page, open the public LinkedIn posts of our interns, read the week-by-week plan for every internship, and email admin@skill-me-intern.in with any question. The stipend and job offers go to top performers only; job offers come from our hiring partners or from SkillMe.

01What are the requirements for this internship?

The entire 4-week internship — tasks, offer letter, and guidance — is free for every student who takes the screening quiz. You work remotely at your own pace, build each week's project, and submit it as a LinkedIn post. There's an optional fee if you want the verified certificate + LOR, which unlocks at the end, once 3 of your 4 weekly milestones are approved.

Screening quiz100% remote
02How is my performance evaluated?

Your performance is tracked through verified milestone submissions and admin reviews. Scores are based on the number of tasks resolved, submission quality, and project completion. Top performers are highlighted to our hiring partners for direct referrals.

Weekly review4 milestones
03What does the placement assistance include?

Top interns get access to our hiring partner network — startups and product companies that actively recruit from SkillMe. Top performers also get direct referrals from our team to relevant openings and a Letter of Recommendation (LOR).

Hiring partnersDirect referrals
04What is the duration and commitment required?

The internship is 4 weeks long, with one project milestone per week. You work at your own pace within your 4-week window. Most students spend 2–4 hours per day on tasks — it's fully remote and flexible.

4 weeksFlexible hours
05Do I need prior experience to apply?

Basic knowledge of your chosen domain is recommended (fundamentals, not expert level). The screening quiz covers basics to make sure you're ready. You don't need prior internship experience — that's exactly what SkillMe provides.

Beginner friendlyDomain basics
06How do the weekly tasks work?

Each week has one milestone on your project, with clear requirements and acceptance criteria in your dashboard. Build it, share it as a LinkedIn post using the caption we provide, and paste the post link into your dashboard. The SkillMe team reviews it and approves the milestone or tells you what to fix.

Verified tasksReal projects
07How is the certificate verified?

Every certificate has a unique ID and QR code. Anyone — a recruiter, college, or employer — can visit our verification page, enter the ID, and see your certificate details including name, domain, issue date, and duration. It's fully tamper-proof and publicly verifiable.

QR verifiableTamper-proof
08Can I do internships in multiple domains?

Absolutely. Each domain is an independent internship with its own timeline, projects, and certificate. Many students do 2–3 domains to build a diverse project portfolio. Each one adds a new set of approved milestones and a new certificate to your profile.

Multi-trackDiverse portfolio

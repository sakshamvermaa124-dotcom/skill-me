Top performing interns in each batch earn a monthly performance stipend of up to ₹10,000. Your score is based on the number of issues resolved, PR quality, code reviews, and difficulty of tasks completed. The top performers each month are automatically considered.

Top interns get access to our hiring partner network — startups and product companies that actively recruit from SkillMe. You also get resume review sessions, mock interview prep, and direct referrals from our team to relevant openings.

The internship is 4 weeks long. Each week, you receive GitHub issues to solve. You work at your own pace within the weekly deadlines. Most students spend 2–4 hours per day on tasks — it's fully remote and flexible.

Basic knowledge of your chosen domain is recommended (fundamentals, not expert level). The screening quiz covers basics to make sure you're ready. You don't need prior internship experience — that's exactly what SkillMe provides.

Each week, you're assigned GitHub issues in your batch's repository. Fork the repo, fix the issue, raise a Pull Request. Our automated tests verify your solution. This is the exact same workflow used at real software companies — it's genuinely valuable experience.

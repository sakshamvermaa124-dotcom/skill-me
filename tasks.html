Internship Overview

Welcome to your internship! Over the next 4 weeks, you will build real-world applications and deploy them. This document contains all the necessary instructions and tasks you need to complete.

Internship Perks

  • Verified Certificate: A publicly verifiable certificate with a unique credential ID.
  • Letter of Recommendation (LOR): A personalized LOR highlighting your domain expertise.
  • Proof of Work Portfolio: Lifetime hosting of your project portfolio to showcase to recruiters.
  • Industry Experience: Hands-on experience building and deploying production-ready applications.

Instructions for Interns

  1. Complete the assigned projects for your domain as listed in the Task List below.
  2. Once a task is complete, create a post or a short video demonstration on LinkedIn.
  3. Tag @SkillMe in your LinkedIn post (make sure to select the official page from the dropdown).
  4. Copy the URL of your published LinkedIn post.
  5. Log in to your SkillMe Student Dashboard and submit the URL for the respective week's task.

Internship Completion Criteria

To be eligible for the internship certificate and all associated perks, participants must complete a minimum of two tasks. Submitting only one task will be considered incomplete and certificates will not be issued.
